About Atelier Forma

The studio behind the spaces.

An interior architecture practice built on light, honest material and an unhurried way of working.

Studio introduction

Interiors with the discipline of architecture.

Atelier Forma is a 34-person studio of architects, interior designers, furniture makers and stylists working across residential, commercial and hospitality projects in 32 cities.

We believe the best interiors are quiet. They do not compete with the people inside them. They give light somewhere to land, make everyday routines easier, and grow more beautiful with use. That takes planning, patience and a clear point of view, which is what we bring to every commission, large or small.

Our studios in Chennai and Bengaluru share a single material library, a single workshop and a single standard: nothing leaves the studio until we would live with it ourselves.

Founder / creative director

Ananya Ramesh

“A great interior is the sum of a thousand small decisions nobody notices. My job is to make sure every one of them is made on purpose.”

Ananya trained as an architect at CEPT University and worked in Ahmedabad and London before founding the studio. She leads concept development on every project, chairs the material library and mentors the studio’s junior designers.

Mission & values

Our mission is to make everyday life more beautiful, and more possible.

  • Considered

    We edit until only what matters remains, then we refine what is left.

  • Honest

    Materials are shown for what they are. No imitation, no shortcuts.

  • Collaborative

    Clients, craftspeople and consultants work as one team, in the same room.

  • Enduring

    We design for the fifteenth year of a room, not the first photograph of it.

How the studio started

Two desks, one lamp and a stubborn idea.

In 2014, Ananya rented a ground-floor room in R.A. Puram with a borrowed drafting table and a single client: a family who wanted their flat to feel like home rather than a showroom.

That project took eleven months and taught the studio its method: listen for weeks, draw slowly, build carefully, and never hand over a room that is not finished.

Materials philosophy

Material has a memory.

We choose materials that improve with age: oiled timber, honed stone, unlacquered brass and lime plaster. They record the life of a home instead of hiding it.

Our library holds more than 600 samples, each tested against Indian light, humidity and daily wear before it is ever specified.

  • Oiled oak
  • Honed limestone
  • Brushed brass
  • Natural linen
