Material & texture

Material Has
a Memory.

From tactile stone to warm timber, every material is selected to create atmosphere, depth and longevity. Hover, tap or focus a sample to explore it.

  • Natural StoneHoned limestone and sandstone with a quiet, mineral softness.
  • MarbleIvory slabs with restrained veining, book-matched where it counts.
  • TimberSolid oak, walnut and teak, oiled to keep the grain visible.
  • BrassHand-brushed fittings that warm and deepen with touch.
  • ConcreteMicro-cement and cast surfaces for calm, sculptural volumes.
  • GlassFluted, tinted and clear glass to filter and borrow light.
  • LinenLoosely woven natural upholstery and drapery with real weight.
  • TerrazzoPoured-in-place terrazzo with chips cut from local stone.
  • Handmade TilesGlazed clay tiles with the variation only hands can make.

Design process

How a space takes shape.

Five stages, each with a clear outcome, so you always know where the project stands and what happens next.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Understanding your lifestyle, needs and vision.

  2. 02

    Define

    Developing the concept, spatial strategy and material direction.

  3. 03

    Design

    Creating detailed layouts, elevations, furniture and lighting concepts.

  4. 04

    Develop

    Refining materials, finishes and construction details.

  5. 05

    Deliver

    Managing execution and bringing the design to life.

Founder’s note

Founder / creative director

A room is finished when nothing more needs to be said.

“I trained as an architect, but I learned to design by watching how families actually use their homes: where the bags land, where the light is lovely at six, where everyone ends up at midnight.”

Ananya founded Atelier Forma in 2014 to bring architectural discipline to interiors. She still leads every concept presentation, and still visits each site at least once a month during construction.

Ananya RameshFounder & Creative Director
