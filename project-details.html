Overview

A home that opens to the sky.

Residential

Area
4,200 sq. ft.

A contemporary home designed around light, greenery and a calm material palette.

The Raman family asked for a house that felt open without feeling exposed, and calm without feeling empty. We placed a planted courtyard at the centre of the plan and arranged every principal room around it, so daylight and monsoon air reach the heart of the home.

Inside, a restrained palette of limestone, teak, brass and lime plaster keeps attention on light and greenery. Joinery is deliberately quiet, with recessed handles, shadow gaps and continuous timber lines that guide the eye.

Design challenge

A deep plot with one street-wall.

The site was 30 feet wide and 140 feet deep, with neighbours on both sides. The brief demanded four bedrooms, a home office and generous entertaining space without losing daylight in the middle of the plan.

Design solution

Carve light out of the centre.

A 200 sq. ft. courtyard, open to the sky and planted with fern and frangipani, brings light and cross-ventilation to every room. Timber jaali screens filter afternoon sun while preserving privacy from neighbouring windows.

The house has always felt like ours. The light does most of the talking.

Meera Raman, client
