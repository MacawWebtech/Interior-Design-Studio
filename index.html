The studio

From Empty Rooms to Meaningful Spaces.

Atelier Forma began in 2014 as a two-person practice in Chennai. We still work the same way: slowly, in person, and with an obsessive interest in how a room feels at four in the afternoon.

Founder and creative director Ananya Ramesh trained as an architect and spent years frustrated by interiors that looked finished but felt unlived in. The studio was built to close that gap. Every project starts with light, proportion and the daily routines of the people who will inhabit it.

Featured materials

Material Has a Memory.

From tactile stone to warm timber, every material is selected to create atmosphere, depth and longevity.

  • 01

    Natural Stone

    Honed limestone and sandstone with a quiet, mineral softness.

  • 02

    Marble

    Ivory slabs with restrained veining, book-matched where it counts.

  • 03

    Timber

    Solid oak, walnut and teak, oiled to keep the grain visible.

  • 04

    Brass

    Hand-brushed fittings that warm and deepen with touch.

  • 05

    Concrete

    Micro-cement and cast surfaces for calm, sculptural volumes.

  • 06

    Glass

    Fluted, tinted and clear glass to filter and borrow light.

  • 07

    Linen

    Loosely woven natural upholstery and drapery with real weight.

  • 08

    Terrazzo

    Poured-in-place terrazzo with chips cut from local stone.

  • 09

    Handmade Tiles

    Glazed clay tiles with the variation only hands can make.
